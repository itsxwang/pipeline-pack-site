Questions

What buyers usually ask.

Are the emails verified?

Every address is checked for syntax and a valid mail exchanger. Published addresses are preferred. Where SMTP proof is unavailable, the row is marked accordingly; inferred addresses are not sold as verified. If an email bounces, it is replaced free for 7 days.

Do you scrape LinkedIn personal profiles?

No. LinkedIn is a manual DM channel using public company pages only. The CSV uses public company sites, published contact addresses and contact forms.

Can I choose a category or market?

Yes. Tell me the category, market and exclusions. I confirm the scope against public evidence before payment. If there are not enough strong rows, I will not take the order.

Are the first lines AI-generated?

They are deterministic quotes from public pages, with the source URL stored next to each line. A model is optional for non-PII copy only; it is not allowed to invent clients, metrics or observations.

How does payment work?

Email the scope, receive a 30-minute crypto invoice, pay the deposit or upfront amount, send the txid, and the pack is delivered within 24 hours of cleared payment. No cards or stablecoins.

What if I need a revision?
